# FlowAssist — Consolidated product brief

**User and problem.** Tier-1 payment specialists cross-check merchant, reference and settlement evidence before responding to tracing requests. The course scenario assumes 12,000 monthly support tickets and 800 merchant accounts. [Problem](01_problem_statement.md) · [Economics](02_investment_memo.md)

**MVP.** Check a scoped synthetic payment, inspect sources, review a cited EN/FR/DE draft and approve the local record or hand over. User feedback led to plain-language statuses, a verified-facts checklist and guidance on the first source to open. Ambiguity, missing evidence and sensitive requests return to a specialist. Autonomous sending, funds movement and live connectors are outside scope. [Design](03_product_brief.md) · [Architecture](04_architecture.md)

**Success.** Show ≥41.2% active time saving against a concurrent manual baseline with QA ≥92/100, grounding precision ≥98%, zero critical escapes and counter-metrics within the S5 limits. The 50-case development regression supports readiness to evaluate. Held-out scoring and a causal effect remain pending. [Metrics](05_metrics.md) · [Experiment](06_07_experiments_operations.md)

**Decision.** Hold live rollout pending independent validation and rollback rehearsal. Head of Payment Operations owns the decision with Product, Engineering, Finance and DPO review. [Launch memo](08_launch_memo.md) · [Specification](spec.md)
