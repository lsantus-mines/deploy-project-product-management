## Interview guide (adapt it — keep it about the past)
1. Tell me about the last time you [did the job / met the problem]. What happened, step by step?
2. What was the hardest part? How long did it take?
3. What did you try to solve it? What did that cost you?
4. When it went wrong, what did you do? Who did you ask?
5. If you could change one thing in that moment, what would it be?
*Avoid:* “Would you use an AI that…?” · “Do you like this idea?” · “How much would you pay?”
